The path of a prompt · three pipelines

Redaction runs first. The models never see a secret.

Every Gemini CLI chat record goes through default.yml. Human prompts then branch into inference.yml when the policy turns inference on.

1 · ParseKeep user and gemini records, flatten content parts into message, map to ECS and gen_ai.*.
2 · Redact19 detector types over message, content, toolCalls and thoughts.
3 · Tool inputRedacted toolCalls args copied into gemini_cli.tool_input.*.
4 · InferEmbedding, then classify.yml, then semantic flags. Human prompts only.
