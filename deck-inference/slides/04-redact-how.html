Redaction · scan everywhere, replace everywhere

One secret, every copy. The value is never stored.

A pasted token can appear four times in one record: in the content parts, in the flattened message, in a tool call's args and in the thoughts. Redaction finds it once and replaces all of them.

01Scan every string in message, gemini_cli.content, toolCalls and thoughts, recursively.
02De-duplicate by value. The first, most specific detector names the type.
03Replace longest values first, everywhere, with [REDACTED:<type>]. A private key goes whole, from its BEGIN banner to its END banner.
04Record gemini_cli.secrets: count, types, and type plus length per finding.
05Fail closed: if redaction cannot run, message becomes [REDACTED:redaction_failed], the other text fields are dropped and the record is tagged secret_redaction_failed.
