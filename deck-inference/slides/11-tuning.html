Tuning · what to change, and where

Every threshold lives in YAML. Every switch lives in the policy.

Policy switches apply on the next agent check-in. The redaction scanner and the inference pipelines are generated from the Claude Code Transcripts sources by tools/port_gemini.py, so a change is made once and ships as a package version.

01 · Policy

Embed, classify, or neither

The integration setting tags each record gemini_cli_embed or gemini_cli_classify. Classify the teams that need it; embed everyone.

Fleet · Enable ML inference
02 · Thresholds

Per model and per pass

Binary thresholds, the agree pair, zero-shot thresholds, flagging and benign labels, moderation corroboration, NER confidence.

classify.yml · tag aggregate · then port_gemini.py
03 · Labels

Plain-English categories

Add or rename zero-shot labels without retraining. Each label adds cost to its pass, and fewer passes cut the ML bill most.

classify.yml · inference_config.labels
04 · Semantic phrases

Describe intent, then measure

Edit the phrases, re-embed them, then score them on the held-out set before you trust a new threshold.

tools/semantic-concepts.json · build_semantic.py · eval_semantic.py
05 · Redaction

Detectors and stop words

Add a pattern to the scan, or a stop word when prose is caught. New types appear in secret:<type> automatically.

default.yml · tag redact-secrets · port_gemini.py --check
06 · Models

Swap a classifier or the embedder

Model IDs are referenced by name. A new embedder needs new phrase vectors and a new threshold, since similarity scales differ.

docs/inference-tuning.md · extras/setup.sh models
