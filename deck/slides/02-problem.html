The problem

Coding agents act on real machines. Nobody is watching.

01 · Blind spot

Gemini CLI runs rm, curl and git push. EDR sees a shell, not the prompt that caused it.

02 · The record deletes itself

Chats sit as JSONL in ~/.gemini/tmp, one laptop at a time, and are removed after 30 days.

03 · No answers

Who uses it, on which projects, at what cost? Did a secret just get pasted into a prompt? Nobody can say.
